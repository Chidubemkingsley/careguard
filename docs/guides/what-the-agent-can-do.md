# What the CareGuard Agent Can Do

CareGuard helps with medication costs, medical bills, and spending records. The seven items below describe the kinds of work caregivers can ask it to do. They are grouped by caregiver task, so one task may involve several steps behind the scenes.

## The agent's seven capabilities

1. **Compare medication prices.** Look up prices at nearby pharmacies and show the lower-cost options. A comparison does not place an order. Pharmacy stock information may be unavailable, so confirm availability before relying on a price.
2. **Check medication combinations.** Look for possible interactions among the medications provided and show findings in the Medications tab. This is an informational check, not medical advice, and a warning does not by itself stop an order.
3. **Review a medical bill.** Retrieve a bill when available and check its charges for possible duplicates, unusual amounts, or other errors. Review the results with the provider; the check is not a decision about what insurance will cover.
4. **Prepare a dispute letter.** Use bill-review findings to prepare a letter for you to review. You decide whether to edit, send, or follow up on it; the agent does not submit a dispute on your behalf.
5. **Order a medication.** Place an order with a pharmacy when you ask it to and the wallet and spending rules allow it. A payment at or above your approval threshold waits in the Approvals tab, subject to the countdown described below.
6. **Pay a medical bill.** Send a bill payment within the spending limits you set. A payment at or above your threshold is held in Approvals; a payment that exceeds a firm spending limit is blocked instead.
7. **Review spending, wallet funds, and medication reminders.** Summarize spending and remaining budgets, report the wallet balance, and show medication reminders for the caregiver to confirm. The agent can record a confirmation you provide, but it cannot know whether a dose was actually taken unless someone tells it.

## What happens on its own

You start a task by asking the agent or choosing an action in the dashboard. It can then gather information, compare prices, check medication combinations, review a bill, prepare a letter, and report spending without asking you to approve each informational step. Some lookups have a small service fee, paid from the agent's wallet.

Payments follow your spending policy:

- A payment within the daily, monthly, and category budgets and below the approval threshold may be completed without a separate approval prompt.
- A payment at or above the threshold appears in the **Approvals** tab instead of being paid immediately. If you do not cancel it before its hold-time countdown ends, it is approved automatically. Set a hold time long enough for you to review it if you want a real opportunity to decide.
- A payment that would exceed a firm spending limit is blocked. It is not made eligible just because it appears in the Approvals tab.
- A drug interaction warning alone does not block an order or send it to Approvals. The payment threshold and spending limits still apply independently.

See [Approval Threshold](approval-threshold.md) and [The Approvals Tab](approvals-tab.md) for how to set and manage payment holds.

## What remains your decision

CareGuard does not diagnose, prescribe, change a medication plan, or replace a pharmacist or clinician. It does not guarantee a pharmacy has an item in stock, determine whether a bill is correct, or send a dispute letter without your action. Check names, amounts, and recommendations before acting, and consult a pharmacist about medication concerns.
