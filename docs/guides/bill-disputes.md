# How to Dispute a Medical Bill

If a CareGuard audit flags a charge, first confirm the details with the provider and insurer. The audit is a screening aid, not a final decision about what your plan covers or what a provider must charge.

## Review the paperwork

1. Compare the flagged item with the provider's itemized bill. Check the service description, billing code, quantity, and date.
2. Compare the bill with the insurer's explanation of benefits, if insurance was billed. The amount billed, the plan's allowed amount, and your responsibility can be different figures.
3. Keep copies of the bill, explanation of benefits, audit report, and any notes or letters you send.

## Ask for a review

Contact the provider's billing office and ask for a written explanation of the flagged charge. For a suspected duplicate, ask whether both entries refer to separate services. For a high charge or possible coding issue, ask what service and billing code support the amount. If insurance is involved, ask the insurer to review its claim decision too.

If you still believe the charge is wrong, send a written dispute to the provider and follow the insurer's appeal instructions, if applicable. Ask about the deadline for filing and whether payment or collections can be paused while the review is underway; do not assume either is automatic. Keep a copy and note when and how you sent it.

The **Dispute** button in the Bills tab can prepare a letter for you to review. **Email Text** opens a copyable message; CareGuard does not send it. Edit the details as needed and send it yourself. See [Sending a Generated Dispute Email](sending-a-dispute-email.md) for those steps.

For help submitting a bill and reading the audit summary, see [How to Submit and Audit a Medical Bill](submitting-a-bill.md). For explanations of the audit labels, see [Bill Audit Findings Explained](bill-audit-findings-explained.md).
