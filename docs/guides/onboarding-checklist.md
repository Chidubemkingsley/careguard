# Caregiver Onboarding Checklist

Complete these steps in order. Items marked **Required** are needed to start using the agent or to complete the related paid task. Optional items can wait until after setup.

## Setup

- [ ] **Required to start:** Open the dashboard and confirm the correct care recipient is selected. The starter setup includes a demonstration recipient; check the name and medication list before running a task. Follow [Getting Started](getting-started-caregiver.md) and review [Care Recipient Profile](care-recipient-profile.md).
- [ ] **Required before allowing payments:** Review the daily, monthly, medication, and bill limits in the **Policy** tab. Adjust them to amounts you are comfortable with, then save the policy. Existing defaults may already be present, but they may not suit your situation. See [Spending Policy for Caregivers](spending-policy-for-caregivers.md).
- [ ] **Required for payment holds:** Choose a caregiver approval threshold and a hold time in the **Policy** tab. Payments at or above the threshold appear in Approvals; if the countdown expires without action, the payment is approved automatically. See [Approval Threshold](approval-threshold.md) and [The Approvals Tab](approvals-tab.md).
- [ ] **Required for paid lookups and payments:** Check which network the wallet uses, then make sure it has enough funds. Paid price, bill, and interaction lookups use a small amount of wallet funds; medication and bill payments need the payment amount as well. On the default test network, tokens have no real-world value. See [Wallet Tab](wallet-tab.md) and [Testnet Explained](testnet-explained.md).

## Optional first checks

- [ ] **Optional:** Run a medication price comparison to see the results without placing an order. See [Getting Started](getting-started-caregiver.md) and [Medications Tab](medications-tab.md).
- [ ] **Optional:** Run a bill review before deciding whether to pay or dispute a charge. See [How to Submit and Audit a Medical Bill](submitting-a-bill.md).
- [ ] **Optional:** Read [What the Agent Can Do](what-the-agent-can-do.md) for the full list of tasks and which actions may happen without a separate approval.

The agent can perform informational checks once the dashboard, profile, and required wallet funds are available. Do not let it make payments until you have reviewed the policy limits and understand the approval countdown.
