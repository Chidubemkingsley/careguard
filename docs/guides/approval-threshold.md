# Caregiver Approval Threshold

The caregiver approval threshold is the amount that determines when a single medication or bill payment is held for review instead of being paid right away. You can change it in the **Policy** tab.

## How the threshold works

The threshold applies to each payment, not to your running total. A payment equal to the threshold, as well as one above it, is held in the **Approvals** tab. For example, with a threshold of $75, a $74 payment may go through automatically if it fits the other limits, while a $75 payment is held.

The threshold does not override the daily, monthly, or category budgets. A payment that would exceed one of those firm limits is blocked rather than sent to Approvals.

## Change the threshold

1. Open the **Policy** tab in the dashboard.
2. Change **Caregiver Approval Threshold ($)** to the amount you want to review individually.
3. Select **Update Policy** and review any confirmation prompt. Increasing a limit requires confirmation; an increase of more than double also requires typing **CONFIRM**.

The dashboard validates the value against the policy's budget limits. Once saved, the updated policy is used for payment checks.

## Choosing an amount

Choose a threshold based on the largest routine payment you would be comfortable having completed without a separate review. A lower amount sends more payments to Approvals and gives you more visibility, but creates more decisions. A higher amount means fewer interruptions, but lets more payments proceed automatically. Start conservatively and adjust after reviewing actual activity.

The threshold is only one part of the decision. A payment held for review has a countdown based on **Hold Time Before Auto-Approval**. If you do nothing until that countdown expires, the payment is approved automatically. Set a hold time long enough for you to see and act on the item if you want caregiver review to be a meaningful checkpoint.

For details about the pending-payment card, countdown, approval, and cancellation, see [The Approvals Tab](approvals-tab.md). For all spending limits, see [Spending Policy for Caregivers](spending-policy-for-caregivers.md).
